NoiseScope for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026
Developer: Kyeongsu Kim
Contact: laplacian618@gmail.com

1. Overview

NoiseScope is an Android application for real-time audio level monitoring and frequency spectrum analysis. Microphone audio is processed locally on the device. The app does not require an account and does not include advertisements or analytics tracking.

2. Microphone access

NoiseScope requests microphone permission when you select Microphone mode and start analysis. The microphone input is used to calculate audio levels (uncalibrated dBFS), display frequency spectra, detect dominant frequencies, and classify audio signals.

Raw audio is processed in temporary memory buffers. NoiseScope does not record audio to a file, upload microphone audio, or share it with the developer or third parties. You can deny or revoke microphone permission using Android settings. Synthetic Signal mode generates test signals within the app and does not use the microphone.

3. Data collection and sharing

NoiseScope does not collect or transmit microphone recordings, audio analysis results, or other personal information to developer-operated servers or third parties. The app does not provide user accounts, advertising networks, analytics tracking, or third-party crash-reporting services.

4. Temporary data and diagnostic logs

Audio samples and analysis results are held temporarily in device memory during use. The app keeps a short rolling history of audio level measurements and session statistics in memory; it does not save or export these measurements as user files.

The application's runtime may write local diagnostic logs containing technical messages, such as display configuration, requested audio sample rate, or errors. These logs may be stored in the application's private cache and Android system logs. They are not intended to contain microphone recordings or personal information and are not intentionally transmitted by NoiseScope to external servers.

5. Data retention and deletion

Temporary microphone buffers are cleared when audio capture stops or closes. Measurement histories and session statistics are not persistently stored by the app. Diagnostic logs may remain temporarily until cleared through Android's normal cache management or by removing application data. You can clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall NoiseScope.

6. Data security

Microphone access is managed by Android's permission system. NoiseScope performs audio analysis locally and does not operate a remote service for storing user recordings or measurements. Local application storage is managed by Android.

7. Children's privacy

NoiseScope does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if NoiseScope's features or data-handling practices change. Any updates will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
